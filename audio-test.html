A separate, consent-based test for a live audio call between two devices.

Share your connection ID only with the person you intend to call. The other participant must accept the incoming call.

🔒 Privacy notice
This page requests microphone access only when you initiate or accept a call. The other participant must accept before audio is shared. A visible call status and End Call button are provided. Do not use this page to listen to people without their knowledge and agreement. No recording feature is included.
